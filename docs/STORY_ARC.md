# STORY_ARC — The Mandate: Herdr (PROPOSAL, not approved)

> Companion to STORY_BIBLE (proposal). Fifteen levels, three episodes. Fictional election-security advisory escalation only; no real elections, no real offices. Beats describe practical information work (verify, interview, cite, file, retract) — never Herdr bindings. Level verbs are distinct story actions; mechanical Herdr contracts come later in LEVEL.md.

## Revelation alternatives (tested; provisional choice settled)

- **A — Chosen: the independent-corroboration trap.** One genuine event, three faces, fixed counting procedure, player's note as the third vote. Clue-consistency: explains the blank origin fields (two different causes: form default + configured strip), the `LT-47B` recurrence (shared root), the truncation artifact (copy), the receipt gaps (three intake dates), and the player's guilt (receipt in hand since L4). Causal test: remove R-2 and F-47-E3 never issues; substitute an actually-independent third observation and the package survives your retraction (the finale must then fail honestly — the design says so).
- **B — Tested, rejected as primary, preserved as motive: good-faith acceleration.** Vane believed the scan justified action and the paperwork merely hurried it. Preserved inside A as his *motive* (stated in his memo language and hearing concurrence) while the *effect* analysis stays adverse. Rejected as the whole answer because acceleration-by-memo cannot explain the profile configuration's selective stripping or the R-3/R-1 body identity — motive explains why, not what.
- **C — Tested, rejected: hostile-actor fabrication.** A real adversary planted the scan as cover. Rejected: script config + self-report within the hour + vendor remediation record make the misfire documented; a fabrication plot would require capabilities the adversary is denied (ordering staff, forging signatures) and would convert the story's stated floor (a real, ownable event) into quicksand. The design keeps the accident genuine and convicts on provenance.

## Counterfactual audit (remove the player)

Without VN-12: R-1 (Nov 10) + R-3 (Nov 13) = two reports. IG-E3-02 needs three. No F-47-E3, no package recommendation, no Nov 24 vote — but also no investigation: Q-118 still filed, still buried by leave order, and the profile stays armed for the next incident. The player's contribution is therefore both the cause of the emergency *and* the only instrument that can kill it (only the author can retract; only the retraction + proof meets the rule). The design makes complicity the price of agency.

## Twist stress test

- Early guesser (spots shared `LT-47B` at L7): rewarded — L10 lets them prove R-1/R-3 common root before seeing the law; L11 confirms the legal meaning.
- "Vane forged it" theorist: cleanly disproven — bodies match because R-3 *is* R-1's copy; signatures verify; the memo orders a profile change, not a forgery. Beck states this at L10.
- "Ellison fabricated the lodging" theorist: C-1 alone is weak by design; it convicts only cross-walked with SOC sent-mail headers + portal logs + the profile diff — the L14 filing forces exactly that cross-walk.
- "There is no threat" misreading: blocked in-fiction — standard measures continue; the advisory stays; the Board says so aloud.
- No hidden mastermind after L11. L13 reinterprets Ellison's absence (credential mechanics + privilege, not victimhood) without adding villains.

## Remaining weaknesses (self-audit)

1. R-3's truncated timestamp (`21:4_`) is a single copy-artifact carrying real weight; if players read it as a typo, L9 must have the profile diff do the heavy lifting — level design must ensure the diff is obtainable, not just described.
2. Cole's surge-week miss is one paragraph deep; a second corroborating artifact (intake queue snapshot) is recommended so her failure reads as workload + normalization rather than planted stupidity.
3. The L9 publish path creates a public preliminary the L14 correction must visibly address; if level scope can't stage the correction concretely (extra retraction step + trust meter), reframe the choice as circulation scope (wire vs cooperative-only) rather than publish vs hold.
4. Vane's courtesy across 15 levels risks monotony; his procedural moves escalate on paper (request → narrow hours → flood → counsel challenge), but no scene forces a live confrontation — consider moving one Adebayo negotiation beat into L7 (already an interview-heavy level) rather than adding new content.
5. E2 (L6–L8) is document-dense with one live interview (Park is L3); the L8 briefing negotiation is the only voice — consider a short Cole intake-call transcript (records request, not new EV) to vary texture.
