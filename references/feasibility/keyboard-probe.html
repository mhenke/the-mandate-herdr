Herdr keyboard feasibility probe

Browser-level shortcuts may never reach this page. Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+Tab, Alt+Tab, Cmd+Q and similar are handled by the browser or OS before any page listener runs. Their absence from the log is the signal: record the chord as intercepted and note what actually happened.
Test in a disposable window. Expect Ctrl+W to close this tab. Do not worry about losing state; that is one of the results. This page cannot test browser-chrome interception, only what a page-level keydown listener receives.
